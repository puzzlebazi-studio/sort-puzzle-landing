Hello! We're **PuzzleBazi Studio**, an independent Iranian studio making colorful puzzle games for mobile: games you learn in seconds and enjoy for ages.

This blog is the place for everything that doesn't fit on [a game's page](studio:#games).

## What you'll find here

- **News:** new versions, new stages and what's coming to the games.
- **Guides:** tips for tricky stages and how each special challenge works, so you never stay stuck.
- **Behind the scenes:** how a game goes from a simple idea to the one on your phone, from designing stages to its colors and sounds.

## Our first game: Sort Puzzle

Sort Puzzle is a color-sorting game: pour colored water from bottle to bottle until each one holds a single color. It starts simple, then special challenges like frozen bottles, locks and bombs really get your brain working.

Sort Puzzle is free on [Cafe Bazaar](https://cafebazaar.ir/app/com.sortpuzzle.game) and [Myket](https://myket.ir/app/com.sortpuzzle.game).

> Sort Puzzle plays without the internet, anywhere, anytime.
