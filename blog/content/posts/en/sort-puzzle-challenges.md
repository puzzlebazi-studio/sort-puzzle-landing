Sort Puzzle's rule is simple: color only pours onto the same color or into an empty bottle, and once every bottle holds a single color, you win the stage. But from the very first world, a new rule turns up every few stages. This guide walks through all [nine special challenges](game:sort-puzzle#mechanics), in the order you meet them.

## 1. Hidden layers (from stage 7)

Each bottle shows only its top layer; the rest are gray "?" until they come to the top. A color you've seen stays seen, even if you undo or restart.

**Tip:** pour first from bottles whose top color has a safe place to go. Every pour reveals another layer and fills in your plan. Save the empty bottle for when you really need it.

## 2. Limited moves (from stage 11)

On challenge stages your moves are counted, and there's little room for mistakes.

![Stage 11: only 14 moves](/posts/sort-puzzle-challenges/challenge-11.webp)

**Tip:** take a few seconds to look at the whole board and plan before your first move. If you run out, you can get 5 more moves.

## 3. Frozen bottle (from stage 14)

The ice has a counter that goes down with every move. Until it reaches zero, nothing goes into the bottle and nothing comes out.

![Stage 14: a frozen bottle with 5 moves on its ice](/posts/sort-puzzle-challenges/frozen-14.webp)

**Tip:** spend your moves on the other bottles while the ice melts, and make room for the colors inside it meanwhile.

## 4. Locked bottle (from stage 18)

The lock has a color. Complete a bottle in that color and the lock opens, for good.

![Stage 18: a bottle with a purple lock](/posts/sort-puzzle-challenges/locked-18.webp)

**Tip:** make the key color your priority. The sooner the lock opens, the more room you have.

## 5. Color ring (from stage 23)

A ringed bottle only takes its ring's color, but any color can be poured out of it.

**Tip:** treat it as that color's final home, and empty out its other colors early.

## 6. Narrow neck (from stage 27)

A narrow neck lets out only one layer per move, even when several layers of one color sit on top of each other.

**Tip:** watch out on move-limited stages: emptying this bottle costs extra moves.

## 7. Stones (from stage 32)

Stones fill a bottle's bottom for good, leaving less room for colors.

**Tip:** think of a stone bottle as a smaller one: a good spot for a color with only a few layers left.

## 8. Bomb (from stage 37)

A bomb carries a fuse counted in moves. Complete or empty its bottle before the fuse runs out!

![Stage 37: a bottle with a bomb](/posts/sort-puzzle-challenges/bombs-37.webp)

**Tip:** deal with the bomb first, then the rest of the board. Once it's defused, forget about it.

## 9. Tight stage (from stage 44)

Only one empty bottle, and no extra bottle to help.

**Tip:** choose every move carefully and use undo: the game starts you off with 5 free undos.

## When challenges team up

After its introduction each challenge keeps coming back, and in later stages two or three sometimes share a stage, like stage 221 with hidden layers, a narrow neck and a stone.

![Stage 221: hidden layers, a narrow neck and a stone](/posts/sort-puzzle-challenges/board-221.webp)

[If you get stuck](game:sort-puzzle#faq), a hint shows the right next move, an extra bottle gives you an empty spot (except on tight stages), and you can always restart the stage.
